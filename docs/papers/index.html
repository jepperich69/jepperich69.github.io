In review · Nature Communications

The Diverging Action-Space: Evolving Rhythms and Changing Habits in Daily Mobility

Jeppe Rich · Using 18 years of Danish travel data (115,338 weekday diaries), we analyze daily action-spaces across five age groups and identify a two-regime divergence between long-run spatial expansion and post-2019 temporal contraction below age 56.
