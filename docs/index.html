Professor of Transport Modelling, Optimization & Econometrics
Department of Technology, Management and Economics · Technical University of Denmark (DTU)

I work at the intersection of stochastic and integer optimization, econometric demand modelling, population synthesis, and sustainable transport systems. Co-founder and Editor-in-Chief (2023–2026) of the Elsevier Journal of Cycling and Micromobility Research, chief designer of the Danish National Transport Model (LTM), and recipient of the 2025 Danish Transport Research Prize.
